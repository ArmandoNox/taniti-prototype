Beaches & Harbor

Relax on sandy beaches or explore the rocky coastline. Visit Merriton Landing's safe harbor.

[IMG]

Rainforest Zip-Lining

Hike through lush tropical rainforests or take a zip-lining tour for an adrenaline rush.

[IMG]

Active Volcano Tours

Guided treks to the mountainous interior to view the island's small active volcano.

[IMG]

Snorkeling & Fishing

Book a chartered fishing boat or rent gear for snorkeling in our clear waters.

[IMG]

Museums & Arts

Visit the local history museum or browse the many art galleries in Taniti City.

[IMG]

Nightlife & Dining

Check out the new dance club, local microbrewery, and various pubs.

[IMG]

Family Entertainment

Rainy day? Visit the arcade, bowling alley, or catch a film at the movie theater.

[IMG]

Helicopter Rides

Get a bird's eye view of the island's diverse terrain and coastline.

Traveler Tips

Holidays: Many attractions close on national holidays. Plan ahead!
Safety: Taniti is safe, but watch for petty crime in busy tourist areas.
Currency: US Dollars accepted everywhere. Major credit cards accepted.
